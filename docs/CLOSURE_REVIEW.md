# Reviewing release, refund and discharge

Use the request queue filters to separate Pending requests by type. Requests are displayed oldest first; the age label uses whole calendar days and is a triage aid, not a contractual deadline.

Before approving:

- Confirm the selected instrument, institution, request type and reason.
- Inspect verification source, reference, date and notes against the supporting documents.
- Confirm Instrument copy, Bank letter and Declaration are present.
- Check the applicable institutional release conditions outside Tenet and confirm the eligibility checkbox.
- Enter decision notes sufficient for another official to understand the outcome.

Release and refund approval require the instrument to have matured. Discharge is not subject to that specific maturity gate, but still requires evidence, complete documents and eligibility confirmation. All decisions require an authorized Approver or Admin distinct from the creator, verifier and requester.

Rejecting a request restores Verified and records the notes. The institution can make a later request with a new reason; the original decision remains in history. Repeating an already-decided request fails rather than recording another decision.

Approval updates the administrative record only. Any actual bank instruction, fund transfer or legal discharge must follow the institution's separate authorized process. Record external references in decision notes without adding passwords or credentials.
